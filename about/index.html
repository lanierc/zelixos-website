About ZelixOS

A clean Linux desktop, built with care and precision.

ZelixOS is an independent, rolling-release Linux distribution based on Arch Linux. Featuring a bespoke monochrome KDE Plasma environment, dedicated in-house utilities, and zero bloatware.

Mission

Simplicity, freedom, and unified design

ZelixOS provides a ready-to-use, cohesive computing environment without the usual bloat. By combining the rolling-release agility of Arch Linux with a thoughtfully customized monochrome KDE Plasma desktop, it delivers a setup that is both aesthetically understated and technically powerful.

The system is tuned to remain responsive and stable across a wide spectrum of modern and legacy hardware—offering users a calm, focused workspace from the moment they boot.

ZelixOS was created by Yasir Peker under CyberPlus Studios to answer a simple need: an Arch-based system that looks beautifully consistent right out of the box without imposing unwanted default apps on the user.

Principles

What guides the system

Monochrome Aesthetics

A unified OLED-friendly dark palette and crisp typography designed to minimize distraction and eye strain.

Clean-Slate Simplicity

No forced browser or bundled suites. The system starts light and lets you install only what you actually need.

Arch Linux Power

Enjoy up-to-date Linux kernels, bleeding-edge packages, and full access to the official Arch repos and AUR.

Bespoke In-House Tools

Equipped with Zelix Installer (`zelixins`), Zelix Updater, Zelix Hello, and CLI utilities for effortless management.
