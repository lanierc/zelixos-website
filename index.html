Features

Bespoke, clean, and powerful

Crafted for users who appreciate minimalism, performance, and complete system control.

Flexible Desktop Choices

Ships with a clean KDE Plasma environment, while the installer lets you choose GNOME, Xfce, Hyprland, Sway, i3, and more.

Arch Linux Core

Bleeding-edge Linux kernel, rolling updates, and full access to the vast Arch User Repository (AUR).

Zelix Installer

Our custom graphical installer (`zelixins`) allows picking your desktop environment, disk partitioning, and bilingual setup.

Zelix Hello

Your post-install welcome hub. Easily install your favorite web browsers, dev tools, and desktop applications.

Zelix Updater

A standalone, lightweight GUI utility for one-click system package updates and maintenance.

Clean-Slate Philosophy

Zero pre-bundled bloatware or forced suites. The system starts light and lets you build your ideal environment.

Zelix Extras Wallpapers

Includes a built-in gallery of 90 curated aesthetic wallpapers ready to apply straight from the desktop settings.

Zelix CLI & Terminal

Includes the `zelix` command-line utility, Fastfetch info display, and pre-configured Zsh/Bash defaults.
