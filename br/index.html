Tips for Helpful Reports

  • • Specify your hardware specs (CPU, GPU, RAM).
  • • Note whether you boot in UEFI or BIOS mode.
  • • Attach logs from terminal or installer screen.
  • • Describe steps to reliably reproduce the bug.
